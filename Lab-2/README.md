\## Reflection



\### 1. Did your estimations reflect the actual effort?

The estimates provided a useful planning baseline. The final sprint was completed with all 29 Story Points shown as done, so the estimates were sufficient for organizing and tracking the work.



\### 2. Was your backlog well-prioritized?

Yes. The High-priority stories were selected first for Sprint 1, while the Medium-priority stories were initially kept for later work. This gave the sprint a clear focus on the higher-priority requirements.



\### 3. How did your simulated sprint align with your plan?

The sprint started with the planned work and progressed through To Do, In Progress, and Done. The final Jira board shows the sprint work completed, although the final scope shown by Jira reached 29 points.



\### 4. What insights did the burndown chart give about your team's capacity?

The burndown chart shows how the remaining Story Points changed during the sprint and provides a comparison with the guideline. The final state reached 0 remaining points, indicating that the recorded sprint workload was completed.
